A Lab is a place to labor (not only of love), experiment, research and share - there is no labor, experiment, research or sharing without the people. A Lab is not a museum for journalists, but a place made by a person for other people Start a small Lab , put it in a box, suitcase, backpack, picnic basket and take it with you. A Mobile Lab it actually allows you to go to people, and not wait for the others to come. Now also on Medium , revised edition. Biosafety and other good practices [ edit ] We have collected a lot of information about DIY-BSL1 and specificially looked at the regulations in Switzerland and how to use the DIY-BSL1 Manual Ecogen to register you lab for level 1 experiments using genetically modified microbes.
