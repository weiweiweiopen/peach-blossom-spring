# HOME MADE 2016

Contents 1 About 2 First days Impressions 3 Date 4 Venue 5 Fee 6 Registration 7 Directions 8 Workshops 9 Program 10 Contact Persons 11 Participants 12 Documentation Projects Homemade 2016 About [ edit ] As always the summercamp is 1 week , 24h/day : DIY, fun, solder, jam, cook, learn, swim, perform, eat, sleep, talk, hack, tan, drink, experiment, party, work, relax definitely the shortest week of the year! But Homemade 2016 is special. The SGMK is celebrating 10 years of soldering. The participants are invited to take part in the preparation of the 10 years SGMK anniversary festival that will happen just after the Homemade week in Zuerich. The participants can present their projects of the homemade week at this festival. First days Impressions [ edit ] Iiäätzete!
