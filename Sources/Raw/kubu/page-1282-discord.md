We are currently using Discord in our online discussions. Discord is a free-to-use service for communities to manage their online discussions and member roles under various topics under #channels. You need to register for the service and we recommend downloading an app for your laptop, smartphone, or tablet for the best user experience. See a screenshot above of the interface. Click the link below to sign-up. 
 Join KUBU discord 
 
 
 When you have registered you see a few channels on the left. Read-only channels for information are #info and #news. Read at least the #info text. Below them, there is #moi-hej-hi channel, where you should introduce yourself and let us know how you want to participate. Our discussion topics vary between financials, exhibitions, programs, house maintenance, and marketing communications to mention a few.
