 What’s softest in the world rushes and runs over what’s hardest in the world. Water and Stone. 
 – Ursula Le Guin, Tao Te Ching, (1997, p.43) 
 The Social Practice Lab at Winchester School of Art, in partnership with KUBU, culture house in Kemiönsaari, Finland , invites you to tune into our Elemental Threads Hybrid Conversation series. 
 Our monthly sessions kick off in March with a focus on the element of water, with each conversation bring us into deeper concord with this vital force, often described as the “matrix of life.” Drawing on a wide range of perspectives, the series spans ocean justice and governance, hydropolitics, the fluid mechanics of computer circuits, to the weaponisation of water and the breath-holding experiences of a free-diver. 
 Conversations with guests are moderated by Prof Teresa Dillon, convenor of the Social Practice Lab, at Winchester School of Art and curator of the Elemental Threads programme.
