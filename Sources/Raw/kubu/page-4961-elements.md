EARTH 
 
 Marking the first in a series of interrelated programmes that take the elements (earth, water, air, fire and aether), as their starting point, The Garden and The Hedge is timed with Kimitoön’s 700th anniversary. The summer programme showcased 35 individual art works, performances, workshops and interventions. Inviting reflection on soil health, boundaries, resilience, and our responsibilities to the living land.

 WATER 
 
 With 2026, our year of water, we seek exhibition, screenings, events and workshop ideas that speak to the form and function of water. As one of the primary life forces, waters spiritual and cultural position has long been recognised. Central to many cultures rituals, myths and stories the power and mystery of water bodies have long captured artists, writer, poets and philosophers thinking.
