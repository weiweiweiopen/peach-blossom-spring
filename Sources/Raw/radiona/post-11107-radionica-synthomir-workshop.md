 Synthomir GIF: Damir Prizmić , Radiona.org (cc) 
 Synthomir je 8-bitni DIY sintesajzer temeljen na ATmega mikrokontroleru, kompatibilan s Arduino razvojnim okruženjem, koji za sintezu zvuka koristi Mozzi library. Omogućuje tri načina rada – ritam mašinu (s loop modom), bas i teremin mode – dok se njegovo minimalističko korisničko sučelje sastoji od samo tri elementa: potenciometra, fotootpornika i tipkala. Takav reducirani pristup osmišljen je kako bi poticao eksperimentiranje te omogućio buduće nadogradnje i modifikacije.

 Kako je krenuo Synthomir? 
 
Synthomir je zamišljen kao DIY elektronički komplet za slaganje i dio je istraživanja  ‘Amplitude slike i zvuka’ koje je Radiona – Udruga za razvoj „uradi sam“ kulture provodila tijekom 2014. godine, uz potporu Zaklade Kultura nova. Riječ je ujedno o prvoj institucionalnoj podršci udruzi nakon njezina formalnog osnivanja. Projekt je u potpunosti open source – izvorni kod, elektronička shema i datoteke za izradu pločica slobodno su dostupni za korištenje, prilagodbu i daljnji razvoj. 
 Elektroničku shemu i zvučni koncept Synthomira razvio je Igor Brkić, dizajn pločice i vizualni koncept potpisuje Damir Prizmić, dok je Deborah Hustić osmislila istraživački okvir projekta i ime sintesajzera. 
 Od svog nastanka, Synthomir je prepoznat i izvan DIY i makerske zajednice. 2016. godine Synthomir je bio izložen na Venecijanskom bijenalu arhitekture u sklopu hrvatskog nastupa te je iste godine dobio posebno priznanje u profesionalnoj selekciji kategorije produkt dizajna Hrvatskog dizajnerskog društva, u sklopu bijenalne Izložbe hrvatskog dizajna. Predstavljen je također na EarZoom Sonic Arts Festival / Music Tech Fest-u u Ljubljani. 
 
Od radionica izdvajamo održavanje iste u sklopu Device_arta 5.015 u Zagrebu, događanja  Cirkulacija2 et al. Access to Tools kod Cirkulacije2 i Aksiome u Ljubljani, u sklopu KLFM programa ‘Radio dan i noć’ u suradnji s UMAS-om u Splitu te na simpoziju Creative Museum u Bologni. Izlagan je na nizu izložbi od kojih izdvajamo ZEZ Festival, festival Napusti normalne naprave Drugog mora u Rijeci, međunarodna izložba WonderLab u Muzeju savremene umetnosti Vojvodine u Novom Sadu, Radioninoj izložbi Zvučni hibridi ­- kreativna sonologija u Tehničkom muzeju Nikola Tesla, Maker Faire Ljubljana, Maker Faire Trondheim. U 2024. godini produkciju Synthomira preuzima start-up Intergalaktik iz Zagreba.

 As part of the celebration marking twelve years since the creation of Radiona’s first synthesizer – Synthomir, a workshop of the same name will be held under the guidance of Igor Brkić on Saturday, January 24, 2026, from 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM, at Radiona. 
 Synthomir is an 8-bit DIY synthesizer based on an ATmega microcontroller, compatible with the Arduino development environment, and using the Mozzi library for sound synthesis. It features three operating modes – a rhythm machine (with loop mode), bass, and theremin mode – while its minimalist user interface consists of only three elements: a potentiometer, a photoresistor, and a push button. This reduced approach was designed to encourage experimentation and enable future upgrades and modifications.

 About the workshop leader:

He works in embedded and web programming, electronic device design, and music. He has participated in numerous exhibitions and projects and has been leading successful workshops for many years, with a special focus on Arduino, creative use of sensors in interactive installations, sound synthesis, 3D modeling in FreeCAD, PCB design in KiCAD, cryptography and privacy, and the design and programming of e-badges. He is the author of many robotic and electronic solutions for museums, exhibitions, domestic and international theatre productions, television shows, and independent artists.

 How did Synthomir begin? 
 
Synthomir was conceived as a DIY electronic kit for assembly and as part of the research project Amplitude of Image and Sound , which Radiona – the Association for the Development of “Do-It-Yourself” Culture – conducted during 2014, with the support of the Kultura Nova Foundation. This was also the first institutional support the association received after its formal founding. The project is fully open source – the source code, electronic schematics, and PCB design files are freely available for use, adaptation, and further development. 
 The electronic schematic and sound concept of Synthomir were developed by Igor Brkić, the PCB design and visual concept were created by Damir Prizmić, while Deborah Hustić conceived the research framework of the project and the name of the synthesizer. 
 Since its inception, Synthomir has been recognized beyond the DIY and makerspace community. In 2016, Synthomir was exhibited at the Venice Architecture Biennale as part of the Croatian national presentation, and in the same year received a Special Mention in the professional selection of the Product Design category by the Croatian Designers Association, within the Croatian Design Exhibition Biennial. It was also presented at the EarZoom Sonic Arts Festival / Music Tech Fest in Ljubljana. 
 Among the workshops and presentations, we highlight its inclusion in Device_art 5.015 in Zagreb, the event Cirkulacija2 et al. Access to Tools organized by Cirkulacija2 and Aksioma in Ljubljana, the KLFM program Radio Day and Night in collaboration with UMAS in Split, and the Creative Museum symposium in Bologna. Synthomir has been exhibited at numerous exhibitions, including ZEZ Festival, Festival Abandon Normal Devices at Drugo more in Rijeka, the international exhibition WonderLab at the Museum of Contemporary Art of Vojvodina in Novi Sad, Radiona’s exhibition Sound Hybrids – Creative Sonology at the Nikola Tesla Technical Museum, Maker Faire Ljubljana, and Maker Faire Trondheim. 
 In 2024 , the production of Synthomir was taken over by the Zagreb-based startup Intergalaktik .
