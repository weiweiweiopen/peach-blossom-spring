U subotu, 28. lipnja 2025. od 13-18h u Radioni se održava program Radiona Repair Café: Neo-Retro s ciljem popravljanja i slaganja novih retro konzoli koje udruga ima u svojoj kolekciji, a koje uključuje rad s DIY Galaksija kitom, Picade konzolom i dr. novim uređajima te nastavak popravljanja Amiga 2000 računala. 
 On Saturday, June 28, 2025, from 1-6 p.m., the Radiona Repair Café: Neo-Retro program is being held in Radiona with the aim of repairing and assembling the new retro consoles that Udurga has in its collection, which includes work with the DIY Galaksija kit, Picade console, other new devices and continued repair of Amiga 2000 computer.
