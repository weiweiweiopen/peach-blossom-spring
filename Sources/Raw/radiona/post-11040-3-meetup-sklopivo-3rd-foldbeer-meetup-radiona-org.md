U ponedjeljak, 15. prosinca 2025. od 19h imamo 3. Meetup SkloPivo za ljubitelje sklopivih bicikala. Imamo različite teme na rasporedu od popravljanja sklopivih bicikala, budućih tema radionica do potencijalno DIY projekta izrade Timegraphera. Vidimo se! 
 
 Da se znamo orijentirati, pls, najavite da dolazite preko Google forme ovdje: https://forms.gle/AD5WrK6mGXhFQ1bZA 
 Podržala Zaklada Kultura Nova 
 //////////////////////////////////////////////// 
 On Monday, December 15, 2025, from 7pm, we have the 3rd FoldBeer Meetup for folding bike enthusiasts. We have various topics on the agenda, from repairing folding bikes, future workshop topics to a potential DIY Timegrapher project. See you there! 
 So that we can find our way around, please announce your arrival via the Google form here: https://forms.gle/AD5WrK6mGXhFQ1bZA
