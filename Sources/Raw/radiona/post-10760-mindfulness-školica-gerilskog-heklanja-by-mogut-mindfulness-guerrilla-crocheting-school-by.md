Tijekom lipnja i srpnja 2025. kao dodatak redovnim aktivnostima sekcija Radione, organiziramo Mindfulness školicu gerilskog heklanja pod vodstvom Tajane Vidojević poznatije pod umjetničkim imenom Mogut – Guardian of Heritage . 
 Foto: Elton John, David Bowie, Freddie Mercury – Feita e Mano  (c) 
 Grupa okuplja hardver hakere, programere, makere, dizajnere, ilustratore i sve koji se žele izraziti iglom i vunom s ciljem smirivanja živaca, jačanja fokusa i generalno kolektivnog zezanja uz solidan humor. 
 Grupa je za sad popunjena, ali ako ste zainteresirani za buduće sudjelovanje pošaljite nam e-mail: radiona.org@gmail.com 
 
 
Foto: VGdanas (c) 
 O voditeljici: 
 
Tajana Vidojević poznata pod umjetničkim imenom Mogut – Guardian of Heritage jedna je badass sjajna žena i samohrana majka koja ima crni pojas heklanja kojim se bavi posljednjih dvadeset godina. Često drži radionice heklanja, a svoje radove redovito izlaže i prodaje na krafterskim i obrtničkim sajmovima i festivalima u zagrebačkom prstenu. 
 //////////////////////////////////////////// 
 During June and July 2025, as an addition to the regular activities of the Radiona sections, we are organising a Mindfulness school of guerilla crochet led by Tajana Vidojević, better known by her stage name Mogut – Guardian of Heritage. 
 The group gathers hardware hackers, programmers, makers, designers, illustrators and everyone who wants to express themselves with a needle and wool with the aim of calming nerves, strengthening focus and general collective banter with solid humor. 
 The group is currently full, but if you are interested in future participation, send us an email: radiona.org@gmail.com 
 About the host: 
 
Tajana Vidojević, known by her stage name Mogut – Guardian of Heritage, is a badass, awesome woman and single mother who has a black belt in crochet, which she has been doing for the past twenty years. She often holds crochet workshops, and regularly exhibits and sells her work at craft and artisan fairs and festivals in the Zagreb ring.
