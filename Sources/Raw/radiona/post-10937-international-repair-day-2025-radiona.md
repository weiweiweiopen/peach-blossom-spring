# International Repair Day 2025 @ Radiona

Source: https://radiona.org/international-repair-day-2025-radiona/

We will mark International Repair Day on Saturday, 18th October from 2pm ongoing in Radiona. We are planning repairing some of the retro devices from our collection, such as computers, cassette players, robots and similar devices. It is an open day for visitors.
