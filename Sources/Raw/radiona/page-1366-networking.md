Radiona.org – Zagreb Makerspace is: 
 A full member and co-founder of Feral Labs Network since 2018 – a network of temporary dislocated hubs for research in art, technology and communities, consist of six partners from six EU countries, who joined in their common interest in art-science research and contemporary do-it-yourself (DIY) & do-it-with-others (DIWO) communities. 
 A full member of the Repair Café International Foundation Network of international Repair Cafés since 2024. 
 A member of the Right to Repair Europe network since 2024. 
 A full member of Clubture since 2015 –  a network connecting independent cultural organizations in Croatia and South East Europe in the fields of contemporary art and urban culture. 
 A full member of LPZ Grad Zagreb – Working Group Local Partnership for Employment of the City of Zagreb – member since September, 2020. 
 PAST CLOSED NETWORKS AND WORKING GROUPS: 
 2019-2022 
 
A full member of working group project Resourceful Cities (UrbReC) -Sustainable Cities as an UrbAct –  Local Working Group founded by the City Office for Economy, Energy and Environmental Protection, City of Zagreb. 
 2012-2014 
 
Associate member of KeyCoNet – the European Policy Network on Key Competences in School Education.
