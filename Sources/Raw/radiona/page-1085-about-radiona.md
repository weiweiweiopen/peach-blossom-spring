 O Radioni 
 Radiona.org – Udruga za razvoj “uradi-sam” kulture je nezavisni art-tech laboratorij, hackerspace i community makerspace sa sjedištem u Zagrebu. Osnovana 2011. godine, Radiona djeluje kao građanski laboratorij u kojem se umjetnost, tehnologija, znanost i društvo susreću kroz eksperimentalnu praksu, otvoreni hardver i suradničko učenje. 
 Radiona okuplja umjetnike, inženjere, dizajnere, istraživače i entuzijaste zainteresirane za istraživanje tehnologije kroz praktičan rad i eksperiment. Organizacija razvija programe koji povezuju medijsku umjetnost, elektroniku, maker kulturu i društvene inovacije, potičući interdisciplinarnu razmjenu između kreativnih i tehničkih zajednica. 
 Od osnutka do danas Radiona je organizirala više od 700 radionica, 50 izložbi, 8 međunarodnih kampova, 8 hackathona te cijeli niz predavanja i javnih događanja , surađujući s umjetnicima, istraživačima i organizacijama iz cijele Europe i šire. Kroz svoje programe i zajedničke inicijative Radiona je uključila tisuće sudionika te doprinijela razvoju aktivnog ekosustava makera, umjetnika, inženjera i učenika zainteresiranih za eksperimentalne tehnologije i otvoreno znanje. 
 Radiona djeluje kao hibridna platforma koja uključuje makerspace i hackerspace, laboratorij za medijsku umjetnost, Repair Café inicijativu te program umjentičkih rezidencija. Kroz radionice, izložbe, istraživačke projekte, rezidencije i javna događanja Radiona potiče učenje kroz praksu te razvija kulturu DIY-a, DIWO-a (Do-It-With-Others) i DITO-a (Do-It-Together). 
 Uz eksperimentalne programe umjetnosti i tehnologije, Radiona razvija inicijative inkluzivnog dizajna i asistivnih tehnologija kroz InkluLAB, program posvećen pristupačnosti, inkluzivnom obrazovanju i zajedničkom razvoju alata i tehnologija za osobe s invaliditetom. 
 Radiona je posebno prepoznata po radu u području kreativne elektronike, kulture sintisajzera, otvorenog hardvera, eksperimentalnih instrumenata, digitalne fabrikacije i hibridnih umjetničkih praksi koje povezuju umjetničko istraživanje s tehnološkim eksperimentom. 
 Edukacija i razmjena znanja nalaze se u središtu Radionine misije. Kroz radionice, rezidencije, community programe, aktivnosti InkluLAB-a i Repair Café inicijativu organizacija promiče neformalno i međugeneracijsko učenje kroz STEAM pristup, pristupačne tehnologije i inicijative koje dolaze iz zajednice. 
 Spajajući umjetnost, tehnologiju, otvoreno znanje i suradničku kulturu, Radiona djeluje kao platforma za eksperiment, razmjenu znanja i izgradnju zajednice unutar šireg europskog ekosustava nezavisnih art-tech laboratorija .

 ENGLISH 
 About Radiona 
 Radiona.org – Association for Development of “Do-It-Yourself” Culture is an independent art-tech lab, hackerspace and community makerspace based in Zagreb, Croatia. Founded in 2011, Radiona operates as a citizen laboratory where art, technology, science and society intersect through experimental practice, open hardware and collaborative learning. 
 Radiona brings together artists, engineers, designers, researchers and enthusiasts interested in exploring technologies through hands-on experimentation. The organisation develops programs that connect media art, electronics, maker culture and social innovation, fostering interdisciplinary exchange between creative and technical communities. 
 Since its founding, Radiona has organised more than 700 workshops, 50 exhibitions, 8 international camps, 8 hackathons , as well as numerous lectures and public events, collaborating with artists, researchers and organisations across Europe and beyond. Through its programs and collaborative initiatives, Radiona has engaged thousands of participants and contributed to the development of an active ecosystem of makers, artists, engineers and learners interested in experimental technologies and open knowledge. 
 Radiona operates as a hybrid platform that includes a makerspace and hackerspace, a media art laboratory, a Repair Café initiative and an artist-in-residence program . Through workshops, exhibitions, research projects, residencies and public events, Radiona encourages learning by doing and supports DIY, DIWO (Do-It-With-Others) and DITO (Do-It-Together) cultures. 
 In addition to its experimental art and technology programs, Radiona develops inclusive design and assistive technology initiatives through InkluLAB , a program dedicated to accessibility, inclusive education and the collaborative development of tools and technologies for people with disabilities. 
 Radiona is particularly recognised for its work in creative electronics, synthesizer culture, open hardware, experimental instruments, digital fabrication and hybrid art practices that connect artistic exploration with technological experimentation. 
 Education and knowledge-sharing are central to Radiona’s mission. Through workshops, residencies, community programs, InkluLAB activities and its Repair Café initiative, the organisation promotes informal and intergenerational learning through STEAM approaches, accessible technologies and community-driven initiatives. 
 By combining art, technology, open knowledge and collaborative culture, Radiona acts as a platform for experimentation, knowledge exchange and community building within the broader European ecosystem of independent art-tech laboratories .
