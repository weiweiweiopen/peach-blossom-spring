SkloPivo je neformalno druženje vlasnika i entuzijasta sklopivih bicikala u Zagrebu! Primijetili smo da nas ima puno po gradu – pa ajmo se družiti. Drugi meetup je u ponedjeljak, 22. rujna 2025. od 20:00h u Radioni (Nova cesta 186). Nalazimo se zbog popravaka i razgovora oko budućih aktivnosti. 
 For English, please scroll down. 
 
 Da se znamo orijentirati, pls, najavite da dolazite preko Google forme ovdje: https://forms.gle/CtXUE7znta8pWDj98 
 Podržala Zaklada Kultura Nova

 SkloPivo / FoldBeer Meetup is an informal gathering of folding bike owners and enthusiasts in Zagreb! We noticed that there are a lot of us around the city – so let’s hang out. The second meetup is on Monday, September 22, 2025, at 8:00 PM at Radiona (Nova cesta 186). We meet for repairs and discussions about future activities. 
 So that we can find our way around, please announce your arrival via the Google form here: https://forms.gle/CtXUE7znta8pWDj98 
 Supported by Kultura Nova Foundation
