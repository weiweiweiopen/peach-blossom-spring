U ponedjeljak, 3. ožujka 2026. s početkom u 19h nastavljamo sa Mindfulness sesijom gerilskog heklanja pod vodstvom Tajane Vidojević poznatije pod umjetničkim imenom  Mogut – Guardian of Heritage . 
 For English please scroll down. 
 
 Ako do sada niste sudjelovali, a htjeli bi doći, šibnite na e-mail na radiona.org@gmail.com 
 Grupa okuplja hardver hakere, programere, makere, dizajnere, ilustratore i sve koji se žele izraziti iglom i vunom s ciljem smirivanja živaca, jačanja fokusa i generalno kolektivnog zezanja uz solidan humor. 
 ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// 
 On Monday, March 3, 2026, starting at 7 p.m., we continue with the Mindfulness session of guerilla crochet led by Tajana Vidojević, better known by the stage name Mogut – Guardian of Heritage. 
 If you haven’t participated so far and would like to come, send us e-mail to radiona.org@gmail.com 
 The group gathers hardware hackers, programmers, makers, designers, illustrators and everyone who wants to express themselves with a needle and wool with the aim of calming nerves, strengthening focus and general collective banter with solid humor.
