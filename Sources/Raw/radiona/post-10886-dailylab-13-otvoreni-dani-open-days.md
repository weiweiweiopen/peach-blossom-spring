Na DailyLab 13 možete doći raditi na svojem projektu, trebate nešto sami popraviti, a nemate prostor ili uređaj poput osciloskopa, pa možete samo skoknuti negdje gdje to možete napraviti. Zanima vas pojedino područje, a upravo vam se otvorila mogućnost da to napravite tijekom dana. 
 
Tome služi DailyLab. 
 
 DailyLab najavljuje se nekoliko dana prije održavanja i prijavljujete se putem Google forme. Idući tjedan DailyLab 13 održava se u utorak, srijedu i četvrtak, 24., 25. i 26. rujna 2025. od 10:00-14:00 sati, a prijaviti se možete ovdje: https://forms.gle/P15cKa4i7TkhBMEJA 
 ENG 
 You can come to DailyLab to work on your project, you need to fix something yourself, and you don’t have space or a device like an oscilloscope, so you can just jump somewhere where you can do it. You are interested in a particular area, and the opportunity to do it during the day has just opened up for you. 
 
That’s what DailyLab is for. 
 DailyLab is announced a few days before the event and you register via Google Forms. Next week, DailyLab 13 will be held on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, September 24th, 25th and 26th, 2025 from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, and you can register here: https://forms.gle/P15cKa4i7TkhBMEJA
