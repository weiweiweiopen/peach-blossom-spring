Paul Grandjean poziva sve zainteresirane za glazbenu produkciju na svoje sesije u Radioni. Ove su sesije namijenjene istraživanju načinima na koji želite stvarati glazbu, bilo da je to techno, ambient, eksperimentalna mjuza ili nešto drugo. Ableton je samo alat — pravi fokus sesija ste vi i vaš zvuk. 
 Photo by Erwi on Unsplash (cc) 
 Za prijavu potrebno je imati popis od 5 pjesama koje vas predstavljaju + 5 žanrova/vrsta glazbe koju biste željeli stvoriti. Nakon što proučim vaše interese, stvaram lekcije usmjerene na vas. 
 Na sesijama ćemo prolaziti područja od uzorkovanja i sinteze do dizajna zvuka i resampliranja, koristeći alate poput Samplera, Simplera, Operatora, Analog, Collisiona i nekoliko mojih omiljenih dodataka – pluginova , trikova i kreativnih tijekova rada ( workflow ). 
 
Naučit ćete kako izgraditi vlastitu zvučnu biblioteku, oblikovati teksture i, što je najvažnije, kako stvarno slušati ono što stvarate. 
 Grupe su male, tako da svatko dobiva vrijeme, prostor i vodstvo za vlastiti stil i tempo. 
 
I za početak — nudim besplatni uvodni tečaj od 2 sata za upoznavanje, sviranje i dobivanje uvida u ono što tečaj nudi. 
 Dođite istraživati, stvarati buku i pronaći svoj zvuk.

 O voditelju: 
 Paul Grandjean (Genetik Dub) rodio se u Parizu, a u Hrvatskoj živi posljednjih desetak godina. Više od dvadeset godina stvara elektroničku glazbu na Abletonu (dub, techno, postrock, IDM, breakcore…). Surađuje s različitim umjetnicima koji poboljšavaju njegovu percepciju glazbenog spektra i nagnali su ga da shvati da postoji beskonačno mnogo načina stvaranja i eksperimentiranja s glazbom. Osim toga, bavi se izradom scenografije i namještaja u domaćem i inozemnom kontekstu.

 Ableton Sessions with Paul @ Radiona 
 These sessions are about exploring how to create the kind of music you want to make, whether it’s techno, ambient, experimental, or something else. 
 Ableton is just the tool — the real focus is you and your sound. 
 I ask a playlist of 5 tracks that represent you + 5 of the music you d like to create. I’m studying it and then make lessons focused on you. 
 We’ll go from sampling and synthesis to sound design and resampling, using tools like Sampler, Simpler, Operator, Analog, Collision and a few of my favorite plugins, tricks, and creative workflows. 
 
You’ll learn how to build your own sound library, shape textures, and most importantly, how to really listen to what you’re creating. 
 Groups are kept small, so everyone gets time, space, and guidance for their own style and pace. 
 
And to kick things off — I’m offering a free 2-hours intro class to meet, jam, and get a taste of what the course is all about. 
 Come explore, make noise, and find your sound.

 About the mentor: 
 
Paul Grandjean (Genetik Dub) is a native of Paris, living in Croatia for the past ten years. He has been creating electronic music on Ableton for more than twenty years (dub, techno, postrock, IDM, breakcore…). He collaborates with various artists who improve his perception of the musical spectrum and have made him realize that there are infinitely many ways to create and experiment with music. In addition, he is engaged in the creation of scenography and furniture in domestic and international contexts.
