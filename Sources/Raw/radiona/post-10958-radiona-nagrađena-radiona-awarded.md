Na ovogodišnjem Festivalu prvih 2025. sudjelovale su Radionine članice Sandra Maglov i Lea Kune u svojstvu su-autorica radova na kojima su po prvi puta sudjelovale u nekom izlagačkom radu našeg kolektiva. Sretni smo što su Sandra i Lea te dva rada Radione nagrađeni na Festivalu prvih 2025. 
 
 Stručni festivalski žiri glavnu je nagradu Festivala dodijelio Sandri Maglov i stručnom timu Radione (Deborah Hustić, Paula Bučar, Goran Mahovlić, Zvonimir Domazet) za rad Objekti i konotacije . 
 
 Nagradu publike dobila je Lea Kuna sa suradnikom Igorom Brkićem za rad RotoMotoMuzik . 
 
 Na ovogodišnjem Festivalu prvih  održanog pod nazivom Kratki spoj sudjelovali smo sa radovima: MAMBO-JUMBO-JACK (Damir Prizmić), PIC boulderDash (Mario Pavlić), FPGA arkadna konzola (Mario Pavlić, Davor Jadrijević) u Galeriji KIC te gore spomenuti radovi RotoMotoMuzik (Lea Kune, Igor Brkić) i Objekti i konotacije (Sandra Maglov, Deborah Hustić, Paula Bučar, Goran Mahovlić, Zvonimir Domazet) predstavljeni u Galeriji Događanja, KNAP. 
 Festival prvih, multidisciplinarni je umjetnički festival, koji se održava dvadeset i pet godina u Zagrebu. Festival prvih pokrenuo je multimedijalni umjetnik Željko Zorica (1957. – 2013.) postavivši ga kao smotru dostignuća stvaratelja različitih umjetničkih i neumjetničkih profila. FP svake godine proglašava središnju temu. Dosadašnje teme bile su: reklamokracija, GMO i biotehnologije, dominacije, mine, društvena odgovornost kapitala, mediji i prava djeteta, besplatno obrazovanje, poslovi sa Suncem, nepoznata mjesta u hrvatskoj umjetnosti 70-ih i 80-ih, nada, društvo razočaranja, depresija, solidarnost, pravo na (po)grešku, izmišljanje tradicije, doba praznine, nestalna ljubav – o krhkosti ljudskih odnosa, potreba za stanom i stambena politika, usamljenost i socijalna izolacija, te vrijeme rizika – nesigurnost kao konstanta, (ne) vidljive žene, gubitak/tugovanje i Kratki spoj – Tehnologija i vizije budućnosti.

 At this year’s Festival of the First 2025, Radiona members Sandra Maglov and Lea Kune participated as co-authors of works on which they participated for the first time in an exhibition work of our collective. We are happy that Sandra and Lea with the two of Radiona’s works were awarded at the Festival of the First 2025. 
 The expert festival jury awarded the Main prize of the Festival to Sandra Maglov and the expert team of Radiona (Deborah Hustić, Paula Bučar, Goran Mahovlić, Zvonimir Domazet) for the work Objects and Connotations. 
 The audience award went to Lea Kuna with her collaborator Igor Brkić for the work RotoMotoMuzik. 
 At this year’s Festival of the Firsts, held under the name Short Connection, we participated with the works: MAMBO-JUMBO-JACK (Damir Prizmić), PIC boulderDash (Mario Pavlić), FPGA arcade console (Mario Pavlić, Davor Jadrijević) in the KIC Gallery and the aforementioned works RotoMotoMuzik (Lea Kune, Igor Brkić) and Objects and connotations (Sandra Maglov, Deborah Hustić, Paula Bučar, Goran Mahovlić, Zvonimir Domazet) presented in the Događanja Gallery, KNAP. 
 The Festival of the Firsts is a multidisciplinary art festival, which has been held in Zagreb for twenty-five years. The Festival of the Firsts was launched by multimedia artist Željko Zorica (1957 – 2013), setting it as a review of the achievements of creators of various artistic and non-artistic profiles. Each year, the FP announces a central theme. Previous topics have been: reclamation, GMOs and biotechnologies, domination, mines, social responsibility of capital, media and children’s rights, free education, dealing with the Sun, unknown places in Croatian art in the 70s and 80s, hope, a society of disappointment, depression, solidarity, the right to (mis)error, inventing tradition, the age of emptiness, fickle love – about the fragility of human relationships, the need for an apartment and housing policy, loneliness and social isolation, and the time of risk – uncertainty as a constant, (in)visible women, loss/grief and Short Circuit – Technology and visions of the future.
