 Meetup: TickTockTalk – we take apart watches, fix time 
 TickTockTalk is an informal, open meet-up for watches, watchmaking and time. We disassemble watches, repair time, learn from each other and exchange tools, knowledge and stories. The first TickTockTalk will be held on Monday, February 16, 2026, starting at 7pm. 
 Short presentations for Monday, 16.02.2026:

 TickTockTalk is an open lab for watches and repairs – DIY watchmaking, mechanisms, gears, winding, balancing and a hacker’s approach to time. 
 
If it works – admire it. If it doesn’t work – take it apart. 
 TickTockTalk is a place for wannabe ‘watchmakers’, hand hackers, the curious, DIY enthusiasts and everyone who is late for a reason. Find your way while there’s still time. 
 Feel free to drop in! See you!
