 Foto: vasco.gram (cc) 
 U svijetu koji proizvodi brže nego što može misliti, upcycling se pojavljuje kao tiha, ali odlučna gesta otpora. Umjesto nove tkanine, biramo već postojeću; umjesto odbacivanja, transformaciju. Upcycling (nadciklaža) nije samo metoda, već način da se uspori ritam hiperprodukcije i ponovno uspostavi odnos s materijalom. 
 Na radionici ćemo raditi s dekama – predmetima topline, sjećanja i trajnosti – i pretvarati ih u odjevne komade: jakne, suknje i druge nosive forme. Kroz rezanje, kombiniranje i ponovno osmišljavanje, deka postaje odjevni predmet, a odjevni predmet postaje nova priča. 
 Polaznici sa sobom trebaju ponijeti  staru deku  koju više ne koriste te  jedan stari odjevni komad  za kombiniranje (poput hoodice, džempera, jakne ili sličnog komada). Radionica je otvorena svima koji žele promišljati modu izvan logike novog, sezonskog i viška – i vlastitim rukama dati materijalu novi život.

 The sewing workshop “Under the blanket in the jacket” under the leadership of fashion designer Vasco will be held in his studio on Trešnjevka on Monday and Tuesday, January 26 and 27, 2026 from 6 p.m. 
 Foto: vasco.gram (cc) 
 In a world that produces faster than it can think, upcycling emerges as a silent but decisive gesture of resistance. Instead of new fabric, we choose an existing one; instead of discarding, transformation. Upcycling is not just a method, but a way to slow down the pace of hyperproduction and re-establish a relationship with the material. 
 In the workshop, we will work with blankets – objects of warmth, memory and durability – and transform them into garments: jackets, skirts and other wearable forms. Through cutting, combining and reinventing, the blanket becomes a garment, and the garment becomes a new story. 
 Participants should bring an old blanket that they no longer use and an old piece of clothing to combine (such as a hoodie, sweater, jacket or similar piece). The workshop is open to anyone who wants to think about fashion beyond the logic of the new, the seasonal and the surplus – and give new life to the material with their own hands.

 About the workshop leader: 
 
Vasco (instagram.com/vasco.gram) – Vasco is a textile and fashion slager based in Zagreb, owner of the eponymous craft founded in 2022. His works are often provocative and daring, designed to encourage reflection and conversation about important social topics. He considers fashion a place of mass enslavement, but also personal emancipation. 
 He has been collaborating with various actors on the cultural and artistic scene for many years. Graduated from the Faculty of Textile Technology, Textile and Fashion Design, Textile Design in 2022. In the last few years, he has exhibited individually or in groups and held workshops at the Nikola Tesla Technical Museum, the 55th Zagreb Salon, Zagreb Design Week, the Museum of Arts and Crafts, the Q Fest festival, and through various projects he has collaborated with Sabmarine, the Academy of Dramatic Arts, etc., and is now working on films and commercials in the costume design sector. 
 He passes on his knowledge through workshops and private lessons. He is a long-time collaborator of Radiona, within whose educational program he works on the textile program.
