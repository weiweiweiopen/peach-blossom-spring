Na DailyLab 11 možete doći raditi na svojem projektu, trebate nešto sami popraviti, a nemate prostor ili uređaj poput osciloskopa, pa možete samo skoknuti negdje gdje to možete napraviti. Zanima vas pojedino područje, a upravo vam se otvorila mogućnost da to napravite tijekom dana. 
 
Tome služi DailyLab. 
 
 DailyLab najavljuje se nekoliko dana prije održavanja i prijavljujete se putem Google forme. Idući tjedan DailyLab 11 održava se u utorak i srijedu, 17. i 18. lipnja 2025. od 10:00-14:00 sati, a prijaviti se možete ovdje: https://forms.gle/33WbptgvuDimAfEa6 
 ENG 
 You can come to DailyLab to work on your project, you need to fix something yourself, and you don’t have space or a device like an oscilloscope, so you can just jump somewhere where you can do it. You are interested in a particular area, and the opportunity to do it during the day has just opened up for you. 
 
That’s what DailyLab is for. 
 DailyLab is announced a few days before the event and you register via Google Forms. Next week, DailyLab 11 will be held on Tuesday and Wednesday, June 17th and 18th, 2025 from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, and you can register here: https://forms.gle/YstUjysBAM5jaTqA9
