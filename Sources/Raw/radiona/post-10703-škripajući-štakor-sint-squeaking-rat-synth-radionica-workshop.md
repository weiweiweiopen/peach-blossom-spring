RADIONICA JE POPUNJENA! HVALA NA RAZUMIJEVANJU! Na radionici pod vodstvom njemačkog umjetnika Alwina Webera gradimo sintesajzer „Škripajući štakor“ koja se održava u četvrtak, 29. svibnja 2025. od 18h u Radioni. 
 
 
„Škripajući štakor“ je samostalni sintisajzer temeljen na dobrom starom CMOS 4093 NAND gate čipu. Uređaj ima četiri oscilatora oscilatora, od toga dva rade kao modulatori, a dva su glavna frekvencija. Raspon zvuka ide od prigušenog basa do uvjerljivog škripanja. 
 Nadalje, razgovaramo o sklopovima i grafici te o tome kako se  dizajnira PCB i proizvodi od DIY razine do velike proizvodnje profi pločica. Također, bavit čemo se time gdje i kako započeti i završiti projekt elektronskog sklopa. 
 Isto tako, imat ćete mogućnost “lemiti lemilicu” – “SillySolderSynth”, temeljen je na Attiny mikrokontroleru i stvara neobiče i čudne zvukove i bipove. Oblikovan je poput lemilice i ultimativni je nerd gadget za svakog wanna be hackera ili graditelja instrumenata. Napravite ga blještavim blještavim uređajem i stavite na lančić ili ga nosite kao elegantan zvučni uređaj na svom pojasu!

 Squeaking Rat Synth – workshop 
 We are building the Squeaking Rat synthesizer at a workshop led by German artist Alwin Weber, which will take place on Thursday, May 29, 2025, from 6 pm at Radiona.org. 
 In the workshop we are building the „Squaking Rat“. Its a standalone synth, based on the good old Cmos 4093 NAND Gate chip. There are 4 oscillators, where two work more as modulators and two are the main frequency. The sound range goes from subby bass till annoying squeaky stuff. 
 Furthermore we talking about Circuits and Graphics and how a PCB is designed and produced from DIY level to a large production scale. Where to start and how to finish a circuit project. 
 You also have the possibility to “solder a solderiron”. “SillySolderSynth”, it´s a based on an attiny microcontroler and creating weird sounds and bleeps. Its shaped like an solderiron and its the ultimate nerd gadget. Make it a bling bling device and put it on a chain or wear it as a stylish sound device on your belt!

 Alwin Weber aka “StöRenfrieD” is a German artist from Dresden. He has been experimenting with sound since 2001, and started working with the circuit bending technique in 2007. The results of his hacking processes lead to the founding of a project and series of concerts and workshops called CircuitCircle (2009). Since then, he has performed and exhibited his installations at numerous festivals in Europe, such as Cynet Art Festival, Ars Electronica, etc. He is also orginiser of several DIY/DIWO electronic music festivals in Dresden.
