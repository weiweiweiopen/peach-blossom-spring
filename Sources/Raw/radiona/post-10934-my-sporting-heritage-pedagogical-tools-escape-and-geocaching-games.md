We have created two games as a part of the Erasmus+ My Sporting Heritage project. Escape game – The Rift and Geocaching Game – The Sport Heritage Trails. 
 More here: https://my-sporting-heritage.eu/pedagogical-tools 
 We will explore more in the following months the connections between sport, heritage, gamification, creativity and innovation. 
 The Escape game in this phase was made by: Deborah Hustić, Damir Prizmić  @radiona_org  and Laurene Bertrand  @museenationaldusport 
 The Geocaching Game was made by: Deborah Hustić, Damir Prizmić (Radiona.org) and Nicolas Mangiapan (Musée National du Sport – Nice). 
 
We will continue to update games according to further inputs and playability. 
 This project relies on the expertise of European researchers  @uva_amsterdam @ecros_off @sportandcitizen  and specialists in digital pedagogy and the sport field (Musée National du Sport, Michael Culture and Radiona). 
 The  My Sporting Heritage  project, funded by the European Union within the  Erasmus+  Sport programme, aims to contribute to the identification, preservation and promotion of a European sporting heritage, that is often under-exposed. 
 This project relies on the expertise of European researchers ( University of Amsterdam – UVA ,  European Cultural Route of Sport Association ,  Think Tank Sport & Citoyenneté ) and specialists in digital pedagogy and the sport field ( Musée National du Sport ,  Michael Culture  and  Radiona.org ).
