Radiona will host eBadge Workshop LAB and Neo-retro Corner II  during the international  Deep Conference 2025 which will took place from 22 to 23 October 2025 in Petrčane near Zadar in the coastal part of Croatia. Goran Mahovlić, Tomislava Borić and Deborah Hustić will be in charge for Radiona activities during the program. Goran Mahovlić designed a special edition of eBadge for this year’s Deep Conference which will participants be able to make and solder during the event. 
 
 The DEEP Conference is a leading conference in the field of digital ethics, privacy, and security. This event brings together researchers, policymakers, industry professionals, and activists to discuss the ethical and societal implications of digital technologies. 
 The conference covers a wide range of topics, including data privacy, algorithmic bias, cybersecurity policy, and online activism. Attendees can expect keynote speeches, panel discussions, interactive workshops, and networking opportunities. 
 More info here:  https://deep-conference.com/
