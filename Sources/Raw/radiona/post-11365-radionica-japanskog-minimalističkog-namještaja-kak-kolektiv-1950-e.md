Radionica japanskog minimalističkog namještaja: KAK kolektiv (1950-e) pod vodstvom produkt dizajnerice Sandre Maglov održava se u subotu i/ili nedjelju, 9. i 10. svibnja 2026. od 15:00 do 20:00 sati u Radioni. Kako izgleda dizajn koji možeš napraviti sam – bez industrije, bez luksuza, samo s dobrim nacrtom i osnovnim alatima? 
 For English please scroll down. 
 
 KAK je bio japanski dizajnerski kolektiv iz 1950-ih vođen od strane arhitekta i industrijskog dizajnera Yoshio Akioke. koji je zagovarao jednostavan, funkcionalan i svima dostupan dizajn. Njihova ideja bila je radikalna za svoje vrijeme: kvalitetan namještaj ne mora biti skup ni nedostupan – ljudi ga mogu sami izrađivati. 
 
Na ovoj radionici radimo upravo to. 
 Polazeći od knjige Ideas for Home Craft , izrađujemo drvenu klupu ili stolicu koristeći osnovne tehnike obrade drva i ručne alate. Fokus je na procesu, razumijevanju materijala i osjećaju da nešto napraviš vlastitim rukama – od početka do kraja. 
 Radimo s alatima: ručna pila, ubodna pila, brusilica, bušilica, čekić i dlijeto. 
 Nije potrebno prethodno iskustvo, samo znatiželja i volja za rad. Radionica je namijenjena svima koje zanima DIY kultura, dizajn, održivost i povratak osnovama kroz praktičan rad i dijeljenje znanja. 
 Radionica traje jedan dan i možete se prijaviti ili za subotu, 9.5.2026. ili za nedjelju, 10.5.2026.

 Japanese Minimalist Furniture Workshop: KAK Collective (1950s) 
 Japanese Minimalist Furniture Workshop: KAK Collective (1950s), led by product designer Sandra Maglov, will take place on Saturday and/or Sunday, May 9 and 10, 2026, from 15:00 to 20:00 at Radiona. What does design look like when you can make it yourself – without industry, without luxury, just with a good plan and basic tools? 
 KAK was a Japanese design collective from the 1950s  led by architect and industrial designer Yoshio Akioka, that promoted simple, functional and universally accessible design. Their idea was radical for its time: quality furniture does not have to be expensive or inaccessible – people can make it themselves. 
 In this workshop, we do exactly that. 
 Starting from the book Ideas for Home Craft, we make a wooden bench or chair using basic woodworking techniques and hand tools. The focus is on the process, understanding the material and the feeling of making something with your own hands – from start to finish. 
 We work with tools: hand saw, jigsaw, sander, drill, hammer and chisel. 
 No prior experience is required, only curiosity and willingness to work. The workshop is intended for anyone interested in DIY culture, design, sustainability and a return to basics through hands-on work and knowledge sharing. 
 The workshop lasts one day and you can register either for Saturday, 8.5.2026, or for Sunday, 9.6.2026.

 About the workshop leader: 
 Sandra Maglov – After completing her Master’s degree at the Study of Design at the Faculty of Architecture in Zagreb, she further developed her practice through work in various fields of design. In 2020, she launched the project DKO. Dizajn kroz obrt is an experimental project focused on the development, design and production of small functional products using today widely available devices such as desktop FDM 3D printers, CNC machines and lasers – all within a limited home environment. She has exhibited independently at many design events in Croatia and abroad. She has been collaborating with Radiona since 2021 through exhibition projects.
