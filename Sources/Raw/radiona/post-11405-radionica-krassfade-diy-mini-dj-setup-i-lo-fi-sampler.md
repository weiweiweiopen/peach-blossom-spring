U ovoj praktičnoj radionici, “krassfade” – DIY mini DJ setup i lo-fi sampler , koja će se održati u četvrtak, 14. svibnja 2026. od 18 sati u Radioni, pod vodstvom Olivera Jaeggia iz Švicarske , sudionici će izraditi modul za crossfade između dva audio izvora pomoću jednog potenciometra – slično kao na DJ mikseru. 
 For English scroll down. 
 
 Uz to, gradit će i hackabilne lo-fi sampling uređaje s looperom , čime se dobiva mali, proširivi DIY DJ setup koji se može nadograđivati dodatnim zvučnim sklopovima. 
 Radionica spaja osnove elektronike, zvuka i eksperimentalne glazbe , te je namijenjena svima koje zanima rad sa zvukom, DIY pristup i izrada vlastitih audio alata. 
 Prijaviti se možete do srijede 13. svibnja 2026. točno u podne preko poveznice: https://forms.gle/aHkwQatY2fTp3fam9 
 Radionica je intergeneracijska i nije potrebno predznanje. Kotizacija iznosi 30 EUR i svoj uređaj nosite kući.

 U svojim live nastupima koristi hardverske sintisajzere i vlastite DIY zvučne sklopove. Na albumu ghost machine contraption radio je ponajviše sa sintisajzerom Roland System 100.

 Bio je predsjednik SGMK-a (2015–2019), a danas je član upravnog odbora. Sudjelovao je i u organizaciji festivala poput Festival der mechatronischen Künste i Flick the World u Zürichu, te vodi radionice na međunarodnim događanjima i u hakerskim prostorima poput Bitwäscherei.

 Workshop “krassfade” 
 In this hands-on workshop, “krassfade” – DIY mini DJ setup and lo-fi sampler, which will take place on Thursday, May 14, 2026 at 6 pm in Radiona, led by Oliver Jaeggio from Switzerland, participants will create a module for crossfading between two audio sources using a single potentiometer – similar to that on a DJ mixer. 
 The center of the workshop is a module that crossfades between 2 audio sources with a single potentiometer like in a DJ mixer. As a sound source participants build also hackable Lo-Fi sampling devices with a looper. Together this acts as a mini DJ setup that can be expanded with other sound circuits. 
 You can register until Wednesday, May 13, 2026 at noon via the link: https://forms.gle/aHkwQatY2fTp3fam9 
 The workshop is intergenerational and no prior knowledge is required. The registration fee is 30 EUR and you take your device home. 
 Oliver Jaeggi is a scientist in the field of environmental chemistry and a producer of electronic music. He has collaborated in many different projects (System der Dinge, omega attraktor, Krystal Transistor Piknik Kvlt) and released his music on tape, CD, vinyl and online. His solo live sets include hardware synthesizer and self-made DIY sound circuits. For the latest album “ghost machine contraption” he mainly worked with a Roland System 100 synthesizer. As a member of the Swiss Mechatronic Art Society(SGMK) he developed und performed workshops for artists, children and schools. Since 2012 he is organizing the SGMK Homemade summercamp, a collaborative work and research week with international participants. During this week projects on sound, robotics or visuals are discussed and realized. From 2015 to 2019 he was president of SGMK and now is still board member. He conducted workshops in the MFRU Festival 2016 in Maribor and a biohacking workshop in BioTehna Lab in Ljubljana. For the 10 years anniversary of SGMK (2016) he was part of the organizing team of the „Festival der mechatronischen Kuenste“. In 2018 and 2019 he was co organizer of the “Flick the world” Festival in Rote Fabrik Zuerich. Since 2021 he is part of the hacker-space “Bitwaescherei” in Zuerich.
