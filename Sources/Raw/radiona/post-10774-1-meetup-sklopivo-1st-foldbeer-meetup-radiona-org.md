SkloPivo je prvo neformalno druženje vlasnika i entuzijasta sklopivih bicikala u Zagrebu! Primijetili smo da nas ima puno po gradu – pa ajmo se upoznati. Prvo druženje 1. srpnja 2025.  od 19:30h u Radioni (Nova cesta 186) je simbolično – datum kad se godina “preklapa”, a i dan će vrlo vjerojatno biti i vruć – taman za pivo i bicikl! 
 For English, please scroll down. 
 Nađemo se da se podružimo i upoznamo, pokažemo svoje bicikle, modove i trikove, podijelimo savjete o održavanju i popravcima, razmijenimo iskustva o vožnji po Zagrebu i šire osmislimo buduće sklopivačke ture, radionice, hackove i pivopive. 
 Nije nam bitno koji sklopivi bicikl vozite – od no-name bajka preko BTwin Decathlona, Rog ponyja, Dahon, Vero, Brompton, Hummingbird, ZiZZO, Tern,  TOKYO Citizen, Vello-a,  iruka Tokyo, Montague MTB, Carrera, Airnimal… što god se da presavinuti bez nasilja. Osim toga, napeto nam je da vidimo što bi mogli s konceptom DIY sklopivog bicikla. 
 Da se znamo orijentirati s pivom i Hidrom, pls, najavite da dolazite preko Google forme ovdje: https://forms.gle/RWnGbD1S4MotjDg89 
 Podržala Zaklada Kultura Nova 
 /////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// 
 FoldBeer is the first informal gathering of folding bike owners and enthusiasts in Zagreb! We noticed that there are a lot of us around the city – so let’s get to know each other. The first gathering on July 1, 2025 from 7:30 PM at Radiona (Nova cesta 186) is symbolic – the date when the year “overlaps”, and the day will most likely be hot – just right for a beer and a bike! 
 Let’s meet to socialize and get to know each other, show off our bikes, mods and tricks, share tips on maintenance and repairs, exchange experiences about riding around Zagreb and beyond, and plan future folding tours, workshops, hacks and beer brews. 
 We don’t care what folding bike you ride – from a no-name bike to a BTwin Decathlon, Rog pony, Dahon, Vero, Brompton, Hummingbird, ZiZZO, Tern, TOKYO Citizen, Vello, iruka Tokyo, Montague MTB, Carrera, Airnimal… whatever can be folded without violence. 
 So that we can orient ourselves with beer and Hidra, pls, give us some details that you are coming via the Google form here: https://forms.gle/RWnGbD1S4MotjDg89 
 Supported by Kultura Nova Foundation
