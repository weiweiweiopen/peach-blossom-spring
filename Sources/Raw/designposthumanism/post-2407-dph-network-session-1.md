# D+PH Network Session #1

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/2407

We are excited to announce that the Design and Posthumanism Network is organizing our first public online event on October 10th, and we are now seeking speakers/presenters to share insights on how design and posthumanism intersect in practice. This event will explore the practical applications and implications of critical posthumanist principles in design, providing a platform for thought leaders and practitioners to exchange ideas. We are looking to invite the network and people who are curious to the event.

We are looking for three engaging presentations. If you have an interesting project or insights in this area and are interested in presenting, we invite you to apply for a speaking opportunity. This is a fantastic chance to showcase your work and contribute to a meaningful discussion.

To apply, please sign up and submit your proposal through the following form by clicking here.

The deadline for submissions is Sunday 25.8.2024.

We hope you already mark your calendars for 10th October between 17-20 CET. We look forward to the possibility of including your unique perspective in our program. We will go through the suggestions together with the event team and see which presentations would make a great combo for our first public event. We are meeting 30th August to plan the agenda.

If you have any questions or require further information, please do not hesitate to contact the event planning team.
