# The Nordic Summit of Species, Aarhus 2025

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/2991

By the howl of the wolf, the wingbeats of the swan, by the whisper of the birch and the trickling of the melting waters we call all friends and defenders of the living world to gather for the Nordic Summit of Species!

We share the planet with a multitude of more-than-human neighbours: Birds, rivers, insects, forests, fungi and beyond. In this summit we come together to listen to our neighbours, to learn from them and advocate for their rights.

The Nordic Summit of Species is a two-day event bringing together civil society organisations that are advocating for rights and representation of nature. Each with their own approach to lending their voices to more-than-human agents. You will meet ambassadors for bumblebees and swans as well as artists, academics and activists advocating for glaciers and rivers. We invite a diverse array of voices and we would love your voice to be there too!

www.summitofspecies.org

Summit program and registration

If you’re interested in joining the summit and participating in the events, tickets are available now. The last day to register your interest is August 22.

For questions, please contact D+PH members Inna Zrajaeva or John Kazior from Feral Malmö or Thomas Laurien.

Aarhus, Denmark, 12-13 September 2025

Organizers: Arternes Ambassadører, Arternas ambassad Viskan-Borås, Snæfellsjökul fyrir forseta, OsloMet, Feral Malmö, Organismen Demokratie, and VIA University College.

Funded by Demos/Nordic Culture Point
