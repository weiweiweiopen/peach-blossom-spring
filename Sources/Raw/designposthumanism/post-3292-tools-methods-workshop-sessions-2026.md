# TOOLS & METHODS 2026

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/3292

More-than-Human Tools & Methods Workshop Sessions 2026 (will be updated regularly)

More-than-Human Tools & Methods is a workshop session series exploring how designers, researchers, and practitioners can engage with more-than-human worlds through concrete tools, methods and practices. The aim of the series is to move posthuman and multispecies thinking from theory into situated, embodied and actionable design practices. 

Across approximately one to two sessions per month, invited speakers share experimental methods, pedagogical approaches and practice-based research that support designing with, for and as part of more-than-human systems. Each session will be announced individually on our website with full details.
