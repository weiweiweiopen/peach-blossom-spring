# Ocean comm/uni/ty Festival 2025

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/2981

Call for participation: Ocean comm/uni/ty Festival 2025, at TBA21–Academy

Ocean comm/uni/ty Festival 2025
June 16–27, 2025 | Online and open to the public. Hosted by TBA21–Academy | Community Curator: Lígia Oliveira, D&PH Network member

This June, following the UN Ocean Conference, TBA21–Academy will host the Ocean comm/uni/ty Festival 2025: a digital gathering where art, ecology, design, and imagination intertwine in service of the ocean and regenerative futures.
Curated as a living digital ecosystem, the festival explores three interwoven themes:

Imagination (seeds): Envisioning possible futures for and with the ocean through speculative and regenerative thinking.

Kinship (blooms): Deepening our connection to the ocean through collective practices like storytelling, listening, contemplation, and shared experience.

Action (harvests): Generating tangible contributions — be it a manifesto, poem, ritual, artwork or a collection — to the Ocean-Archive.org and beyond.

Contributions may include talks, workshops, listening sessions, poetic gestures, digital rituals or experimental formats. Honoraria of €150 are available for selected contributors.

Call for participation deadline: Sunday, 18 May 2025
