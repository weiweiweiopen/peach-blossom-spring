# Snæfellsjökul fyrir forseta | Snæfellsjökul for president

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/2056

Can a Glacier Really Be President?

According to the legislation, the Icelandic president must be at least 35 years old ✓, be a citizen of Iceland ✓, have no criminal record ✓, and have 1,500-3,000 signatures from people in all quadrants of the country supporting the candidate’s nomination.

Let us unite in casting our votes for a leader that transcends politics and speaks to the very soul of our nation. Snæfellsjökull is not just a landmark, but also a guardian and a symbol of our resilience and strength.

​Why Should We Elect a Glacier for President?

​1. Stability in the Face of Challenges

Glaciers have weathered the test of time, standing tall amidst storms and challenges. They symbolise stability and perseverance, essential qualities for navigating the complexities of modern governance.

​2. Environmental Advocacy

With the climate crisis, biodiversity loss, and the Reykjanes Fires destabilising our daily lives, who better to champion environmental causes than the glacier? Snæfellsjökull is a beacon for sustainability, resilience, and a cultural icon urging us to protect Iceland’s natural beauty for generations to come.

​3. Global Recognition

Nominating a glacier for President will put Iceland on the global stage in a unique and positive light. Our nation will be seen as a pioneer in embracing nature-inspired leadership, fostering international collaborations for a greener, more sustainable planet.

​4. Cultural Symbolism

Glaciers are an integral part of our cultural heritage. Electing a glacier as President is a celebration of our roots, connecting us to the land and reminding us of the importance of preserving our unique identity.

​5. Inclusive Leadership

A glacier for President represents inclusivity, as glaciers are formed by the contributions of various elements. This symbolises the strength that comes from unity, diversity, and collaboration.

Vote Snæfellsjökull—Your Glacial Guardian!

Together, let’s forge a path to a brighter tomorrow with Snæfellsjökull as our visionary president. It’s time to elect a leader whose wisdom supports our interdependence and sustainability. Vote Snæfellsjökull for President!
