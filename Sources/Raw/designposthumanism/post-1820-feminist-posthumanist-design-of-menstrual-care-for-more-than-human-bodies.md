# Feminist Posthumanist Design of Menstrual Care for More-than-Human Bodies

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/1820

In their latest CHI 2023 paper design+posthumanism members Marie Louise Juul Søndergaard and Nadia Campo Woytuk explore how communities can come together to make biodegradable menstrual products. Their research project, Biomenstrual, explore the relations between menstrual care and the environment, and contributes towards feminist posthumanist design methodologies.

Their publication “Feminist Posthumanist Design of Menstrual Care for More-than-Human Bodies” can be downloaded Open Access here: https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3544548.3581083 and a video presentation here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r9TaL5jPR50 

by Marie Louise Juul Søndergaard and Nadia Campo Woytuk
