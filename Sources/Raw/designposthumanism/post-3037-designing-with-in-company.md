# Designing-With, in Company

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/3037

Screenshot

The board members Johanna Mehl, Sven Quadflieg, Satu Samira Hamed and Sebastian Gatz have published an article about the Design+Posthumanism Network in Interactions Magazine. 

It features two projects from members of the network: “metamorphic“, by Yuta Ikeya, and “haunted waters“, by Nonhuman Nonsense.

Read the article here.

The article is part of the reoccurring forum “More-than-Human design in Practice”.
