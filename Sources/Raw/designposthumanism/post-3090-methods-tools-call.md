# Methods & Tools Call

Source: https://designandposthumanism.org/archives/3090

The Design and Posthumanism Network is excited to announce that we are starting to collect more-than-human design methods and tools. Our aim is to gather specific methods as well as ‘methods-to-be’, and to organise open-ended ideation workshops and discussions on more-than-human design methods starting this September.

The upcoming workshops will serve as platforms for shared methodological reflection through the lenses of the more-than-human and critical posthumanism.

We also aim to share these methods as widely as possible, both within the network and beyond. Methods may include, but are not limited to, practices that guide us in shifting toward more-than-human ways of empathising, understanding, creating, caring and intervening.

Do you have a method or tool you believe in and would like to share?

Please fill in the form by 15th September 2025
