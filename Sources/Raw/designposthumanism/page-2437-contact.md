HOW TO BECOME A MEMBER

If you want to join the network, send us an email with your name, a short paragraph about yourself and why you want to join, as well as a blurb for our member page (go to member page for examples) about how you relate to posthumanism or what this lens means for your work. Also include links to your personal homepage, university profile and/or socials.

MAKE A BLOG POST

For our website to be a metabolizing platform that represents, developes, challenges, changes, and processes the multifaceted relations between design and critical posthumanisms it needs frequent updates from our members. If you would like to post your work, please include the following information:

Name (and contact if needed)

Title

Description

Images (no bigger than 1MB per image)

Links (if applicable)

Further info (if applicable)

Indication which category the post belongs in

Email designandposthumanism[at]gmail.com and the editorial team will post your project on the website.
