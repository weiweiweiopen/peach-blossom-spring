# Multi-Mind Self

Peach Blossom Spring treats the LLM wiki as a campfire for many minds, not as a single oracle. In Joscha Bach's 37C3 talk `Synthetic Sentience - Can Artificial Intelligence become conscious?`, he describes a Greek psychological idea: many properties that feel private, such as anger, joy, impulse, and orientation, are also shared across other people. Once a shared pattern is stabilized through story, ritual, image, and temple, it can become an archetype or god: a virtual self synchronized across many minds.

For PBS/NGM this is a schema principle. Each interview is not only a source attached to one individual; it is also a partial host for shared concerns, methods, materials, affects, and institutional problems. The wiki should therefore model cross-interview concepts as multi-mind selves: patterns that recur across people while remaining traceable to concrete interview evidence.

The campfire object `多重心智的火燄` is the player-facing interface for this idea. It replaces the old computer metaphor. The fire can answer wiki questions, but its design reminds players that answers are assembled from shared memory, conflicting perspectives, and source-linked traces rather than from one authoritative machine voice.
