- You can design, print and etch your own PCBs from a flexible sheet of Kapton coated with a thin layer of copper. To do this yourself you need some special materials and equipment, and if you are not planning on etching circuits more regularly then it can be nice to start by looking for a local space that has an etching setup you can use. [1]
- Photo of a flex circuit etched using the exact same process as described bellow in step-by-step detail. [1]
- The following flex circuit was etched at Machwerk in Wedding/Berlin. [1]
- Laser-print your design (don’t for get to mirror it!) onto glossy paper. Catalog paper works really well. Wipe the copper surface of your flex circuit material clean with some acetone. It is important that all surfaces stay clean from grease and dirt. Even the sheets of catalog paper you are printing on. [1]
