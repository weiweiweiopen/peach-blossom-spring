- (haiku from Kate Fletcher’s Craft of Use, p20) [1]
- This post covers a few different ways you can construct your own electrical “pushbutton” or embed a ready-made mechanical pushbutton (also known as momentary switch) into a textile button. What is nice about the mecanical mechanism is that it provides a haptic (and also sonic) feedback to let you know that the contact was made. In future versions of this design, I would like to find a way to construct the mechanical haptic feedback myself. [1]
- There is also another post covering how to make Pressure Sensor Buttons >> http://www.kobakant.at/DIY/?p=7334 [1]
