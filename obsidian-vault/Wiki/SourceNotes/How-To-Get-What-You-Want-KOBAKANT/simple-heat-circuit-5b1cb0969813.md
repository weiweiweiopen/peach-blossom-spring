- You can make a simpler heat controlling circuit using TIP122 transistor. [1]
- The above image is the schematic. If you want to control a lot of heat lines, you can use shift register between Arduino and input pin of TIP122. [1]
- This method is not good if you turn on/off the heat in high frequency. TIP122 will heat up and eventually break if you do so. Also, TIP122 is for 1A max. So, if you are letting through a lot of current, it is better to use this circuit instead. [1]
- The schematic says 3-9V for the heat power supply. This is just an example. If the current that goes through TIP122 does not exceed 1A, you can use bigger voltage (or smaller voltage) [1]
