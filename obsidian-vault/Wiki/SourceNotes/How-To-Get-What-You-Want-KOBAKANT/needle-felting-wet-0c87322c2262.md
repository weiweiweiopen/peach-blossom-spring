- Here is the documentation of my first experience on wet needle felting. My friend who does a lot of felting showed me how to do it. It seems that there are many ways to felt, so this is not the only way, but it works fine. [1]
- We used felting wool (you can purchase it at craft stores). It comes with many colors.. find your favorite combination. [1]
- Felting needle can come in different shapes. Here we had 1 needle version and 3 needle version. The needle has a lot of sharp bumps to hook the wool. [1]
- Place a thin layer of felting wool. Make it into the shape you like to have. It shrinks quite a bit when you felt, so make sure to spread it big enough. then i placed some conductive threads. This is because I wanted to make a test if we can mix conductive materials in felt (and it works!) Place the top layer to embed the conductive thread in the felt. Then I added the little purple cross as a decoration. [1]
