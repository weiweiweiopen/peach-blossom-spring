- When you are controlling actuator that needs more than 5V or more than 40mA (this is the maximum current digital pins can supply), you can not directly drive this actuator from the digital pins. [1]
- This is the case, when you want to use strong motor, embroidered speaker, SMA or any heat actuators.. and many more. You will need to build a transistor switch circuit to control these applications from Arduino. [1]
