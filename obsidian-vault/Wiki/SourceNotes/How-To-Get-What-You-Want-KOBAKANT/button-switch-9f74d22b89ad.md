- A metal clothing button and a button hole that works as state switch. When it is fastened, the connection is made, and if you open the button, the connection gets broken. [1]
- *This sensor is inspired by the Swatch exchange sample “Button Switch” by Ricardo O’Nascimento [1]
- Cut a hole on the fabric. Make sure the hole is big enough for the button you have. You can use non-metal (non-conductive) button too, but I think it is better to use metal button to have stable connection. [1]
- Sew the button hole with conductive thread. In this example I used Statex silver plated 4 ply thread as it is nicely thick and soft. Stitch around the hole with plain stitch first. Then stitch the edges of the hole by wrapping around the open edges. [1]
