- This workshop aims to be an introduction into soft fabric electronic circuits and a DIY concept of wearable technology. Both in our own projects and in workshops we choose to introduce affordable, available, off-the-shelf materials, basic techniques and detailed instructions, so that workshop participants can continue to develop their own ideas and solutions at home. We believe that sewing, soldering and experimenting with materials is not hard and should become everyday practice. [1]
- Since the workshop takes place during the Ying Gao exhibition at the Plug.in gallery, we decided to chose a circuit with mechanical output (as supposed to light or sound). The circuit we chose is the same as Derstrudel uses for their Overheadbots. A small solar cell charges one or two large capacitors, which then naturally discharge, powering a motor. Depending on the amount of sun, the motor will run constantly or in bursts. [1]
- Using this circuit to convey our soft circuit methods and techniques, we would also like to leave creative space for individual expression and design.
