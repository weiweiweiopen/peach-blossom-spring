- June 26th – 29th during the NODE Festival in Frankfurt, Germany [1]
- Not a workshop, but a participatory performance. The VVunsch VVerkstatt is an installation/intervention that deals with making wishes come true. [1]
- Throughout the NODE festival the VVunsch VVerkstatt will provide a place for discussing the role of technology as a tool for making wishes come true. Any NODE visitor can participate in this serious wish making activity and use their skills to make somebody else’s wish come true. [1]
- To create the atmosphere of the werkstatt (engl. workshop), the VVunsch VVerkstatt will be equipped with work benches, tools and materials to build with. Participants will be provided with a VVerkkittel (workwear) in order to play the role of the handVVerker (maker). [1]
