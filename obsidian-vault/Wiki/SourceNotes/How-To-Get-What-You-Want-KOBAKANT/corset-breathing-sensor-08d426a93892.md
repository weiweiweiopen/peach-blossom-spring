- This corset sensor was made for a performance of the Helix by Michela Pelusio [1]
- It uses eeonyx stretch fabric as stretch sensor to detect the movement of one’s stomach when breathing in. On this corset, there are 3 strips to take different part of the stomach. This is due to the fit of the corset, it changes which part of the corset stretches the most when breathing. [1]
- I have tried with one long stretch fabric on the entire front stretch part, but as you get a lot of parallel resistance, it does not detect good. It was the best when I cut the eeonyx fabric into small pieces and apply as few separate sensors. [1]
- Then the sensor was read with Arduino lilypad and sent to Helix controller Arduino via Xbee. [1]
