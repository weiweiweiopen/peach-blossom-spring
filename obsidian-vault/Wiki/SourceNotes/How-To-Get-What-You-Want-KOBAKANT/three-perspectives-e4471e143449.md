- Sketching interactions for young audiences [1]
- Young theater audiences enjoy participatory forms of theater that allow them to engage with content in interactive and playful ways. We want to get to know this young audience and understand how they engage with interactive technology and live actors in theatrical settings. [1]
- Over the course of a 3-day kick-off workshop, 4 weeks of exploration and 1 week of rehearsal, students will work together with the art collective KOBAKANT to produce three participatory sketches – “Three Perspectives” – that explore what roles interactive digital technologies and live actors can take on in participatory performances. [1]
- We will be working with a cast of 2-3 live actors and explore human touch and sound as primary forms of input and output to a digital system. We will receive input on ‘collective work with interviews’ from Anja Kerkerschkewicz and make a short research with school pupils. We will have access to a “Premierenklasse” of school students who will accompany our process and join multiple playtests to give us feedback on how our creations are experienced and perceived by young audiences. [1]
