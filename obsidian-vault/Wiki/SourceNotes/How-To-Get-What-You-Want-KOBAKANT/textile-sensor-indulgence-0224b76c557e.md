- In this 3-evening workshop we will introduce a palette of conductive fabrics, fibers and threads from which you can construct all kinds of textile sensors. We will demand you be rigorous about investigating the conductive, resistive and piezoresistive properties of these materials. We will challenge you to hone your craft skills by producing well-made replicas of select designs. Finally, we will ask you to be inventive and produce and document a textile sensor design of your own. [1]
