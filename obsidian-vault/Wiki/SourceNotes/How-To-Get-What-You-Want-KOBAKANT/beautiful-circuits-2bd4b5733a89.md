- This very short intro workshop to the free and open source Printed Circuit Board (PCB) design software KiCAD (Version6) focuses on how to design beautiful PCBs. Beautiful in the aesthetic sense, paying attention to composition, layout, shape…. incorporating drawing tools and work-arounds to create shapes and layouts that have aesthetic as well as functional aspirations. Beautiful in the caring sense, paying attention to how PCBs are produced and what materials and human resources your PCB will consume. [1]
