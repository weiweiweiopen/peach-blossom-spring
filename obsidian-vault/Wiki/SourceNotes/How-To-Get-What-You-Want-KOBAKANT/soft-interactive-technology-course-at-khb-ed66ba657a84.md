- This workshop is only open to KHB students [1]
- This is a series of 4 workshops to learn how one can move textile surface with electricity. In the first workshop, participants will learn how to build flap wings and flip dots that moves with electromagnets. The second workshop is about experimenting with Shape memory alloy and smocking and pleating of fabric surface. The third and the fourth workshop is to learn how to connect them with microcontrollers (Arduino) and control them with sensors or simple animation sequences. [1]
