## Evidence

- Tips on how to improve your electrical contacts between various materials. It is always good practice to test a certain connecting technique before applying it to more than one connection. [1]
- Between conductive thread and a LilyPad and other sewable PCB [1]
- Use a squishy material on the back of your LilyPad/PCB. Neoprene, foam and felt work well. Fuse it to the base material or to the back of the LilyPad or just hold it in place. [1]
- Stretch conductive fabric is really nice to use for sewing to because it is soft and fury/hairy and slightly squishy on a minute scale so that the hairs nicely entwine with the conductive thread and make for better contact than other conductive fabrics that tend to be stiffer, especially when fused. If you are using fusible interfacing on your conductive fabric you should also be able to fuse the fabric on top of a few stitches of conductive thread and maintain a good connection. [1]
