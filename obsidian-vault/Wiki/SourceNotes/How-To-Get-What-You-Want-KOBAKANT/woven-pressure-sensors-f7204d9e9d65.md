- Just like Felted Pompom Pressure Sensor and felted crochet pressure sensor, it also works as pressure sensor when conductive yarns are woven into a textile structure. Here I tried two types of woven structure, satin and honeycomb. [1]
- The honeycomb is a structure often used for tea towels and blankets. It creates 3D structure when taken off from the loom. This 3D structure helps for the pressure sensor to function as it separates the yarn when not pressured, and it also gives a nice bouncy feedback when you touch/pressure. [1]
- Here is the 8 shaft construction for the honeycomb. [1]
- The try-out movie shows the resistance change between 66k ohm to 8k ohm as I step onto it. [1]
