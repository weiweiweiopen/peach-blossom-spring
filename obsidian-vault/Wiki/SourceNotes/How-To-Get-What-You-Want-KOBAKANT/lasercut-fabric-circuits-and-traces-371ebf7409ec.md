- Fabric PCB’s are flexible, washable (hopefully) and sewable, even with sewing machines. The method we used is basically following the fabric PCB from Leah Buechley. You need a laser cutter to do this method, since the cut pieces are quite small and needs to be accurate. It will be quite difficult to cut this by hand. [1]
- We made 16 pin DIP breakout which you can mount 4051 multiplexer, or any chip which has the same footprint will fit. [1]
- As a result, it works wonderfully! ..At least for the moment. we have to check how it changes the electrical property over time with touching/wearing/washing.We will do the wash and wear test to see if it is actually useable for full scale wearable projects. [1]
- For more detail on each conductive fabric, check CONDUCTIVE FABRICS post [1]
