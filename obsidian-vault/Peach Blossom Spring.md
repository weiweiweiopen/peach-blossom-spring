- Raw evidence stays in `Sources/`.
- Semantic and Entity Layers stay as bridge indexes.
- Durable shared memory lives in `Wiki/Concepts`, `Methods`, `Materials`, `Theories`, `SocialForms`, `Projects`, `Comparisons`, and `Syntheses`.
